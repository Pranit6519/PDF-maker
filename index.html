Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Neat PDF Maker

Is Neat PDF Maker completely free?

Yes. Neat PDF Maker is 100% free with no hidden fees, no watermarks, and no account required — free forever.

Do my images get uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely inside your browser. Your files never leave your device and are never sent to any server.

What image formats are supported?

Neat PDF Maker supports JPG, PNG, and WEBP formats. You can upload multiple images at once to create a multi-page PDF.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. After the first page load, Neat PDF Maker works completely offline. No internet is needed to generate or download your PDF.

What PDF size does it create?

It creates standard A4 PDFs (210mm × 297mm) — the format used for school and college submissions across India and worldwide.

Can I add a cover page with my student details?

Yes. Enable Assignment Mode to add a professional cover page with your name, class, subject, roll number, teacher name, and today's date.

How many images can I convert at once?

There is no strict limit. Upload as many images as needed — each becomes one page. You can reorder or remove pages before generating the PDF.

How do I convert images to PDF?

Upload your images, arrange the pages, optionally fill in your assignment details, then click Generate PDF. Your clean A4 PDF downloads instantly.
